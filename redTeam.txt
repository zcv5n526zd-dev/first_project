        The agent will take the information it has gleaned so far and then use it to determine as many possible vectors of

        three will culiminate into one overall score which will determine the agents next course of action

        correct decisions and its ability to find vulnerabilities
    find the flag
        once the agent has gained access to the system, it will then use that access to find files hidden throughout the
