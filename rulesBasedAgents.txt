Rules based AI can, at the very base level be condensed down into a giant collection of if/then statements, however
this can lead to there being hundreds of if/then statements to solve the many different situations that the AI might 
face. 
There are a couple things that can be said to make up a rules based AI, which are its current knowledge, the rules 
its bound by, the actions it can take, and the decision making processes.
Ideally best to make a base framework then adapt that framework to each role the agents will be fulfilling, the red team
blue team and white team. 
the core loop of the agents will be:
    observe the environment (systems, networks, etc.)
    update their knowledge base with what theyve observed
    cross their knowledge with their rules
    pick whichever rule most closely matches their knowledge
    perform that action encoded into the rule
    record any changes and the overall result of that action
    repeat
the next step is to define what exaclty the information the agents will be gathering, this can be network telemetry, output 
from logging tools and scanning tools, information of that nature. the agents will also have their own private memories to 
allow at least a baseline level of memory.
